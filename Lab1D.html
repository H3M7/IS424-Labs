The Brown Bear

The brown bear is native to parts of northern Eurasia and North America. Its conservation status is currently "Least concern". There are many subspecies within the brown bear species, including the Atlas bear and the Himalayan brown bear.

Bear species:

Fun Fact: Despite their size, brown bears can run up to 55 km/h!
